The tier list maker sorts games into rows instead of numbering them. It starts with the classic S, A, B, C and D, but every row can be renamed, recolored, moved or deleted.

**[Open the tier list maker →](https://my9games.cc/tier)** (or the **Tier List** tab on the homepage)

## 1. Add games

Use the yellow search bar. It understands the same shortcuts as the grid maker (`botw`, `ff7`, `melee`, even `skyrm`). Each game you pick lands in **Not ranked yet**, below the tiers, until you place it.

Rank a series, a console, your year, a genre: up to 100 games fit in one list.

## 2. Place them

- **Computer**: drag a cover into a tier. Drag inside a row to change the order.
- **Phone**: tap a cover, then tap the tier it belongs in.

![A tier list in progress: some games ranked, the rest waiting in Not ranked yet]({{ '/assets/img/tier-ranked.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="904" height="1011" loading="lazy"}

## 3. Make the tiers your own

Tap a tier's label (the colored S, A, B…) to open **Edit tier**:

![The Edit tier panel]({{ '/assets/img/tier-edit.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="420" height="308" loading="lazy"}

- **Name**: type anything short. "Peak", "Mid", "Never finished", "Why did I buy this".
- **Color**: pick a swatch, or the last one for a custom color.
- **Move up / Move down**: reorder the rows.
- **+ Tier below**: insert a new row right under this one.
- **Clear row**: send its games back to Not ranked yet.
- **Delete tier**: remove the row; its games also go back to Not ranked yet.

**+ Add a tier** under the board adds a row at the bottom. A list can have up to **eight** tiers. **Reset** starts over.

## 4. Start from your Steam library

Ranking from memory means forgetting half of what you played. Press **Import from Steam** under the board and paste your profile link (see [Steam import](../steam-import/) for making it public). Your **30 most played games** drop into Not ranked yet, most played first, with hours on each cover. The tiers stay empty: the ranking is your job.

![Thirty Steam games waiting in Not ranked yet]({{ '/assets/img/tier-steam-pool.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="904" height="1092" loading="lazy"}

**Show Steam hours** controls whether the hours appear on the covers and in the image.

## 5. Export and share

- **Download image** draws a clean picture: your title on top, every tier in its color, real cover art. It is rendered at double resolution (2160 pixels wide), so it stays sharp when people zoom in on a phone.
- **Copy link** copies a link that contains the whole list. Nothing is stored on a server, so the link never expires, and its preview on X, Discord or iMessage shows your list.
- Your list is also saved in the page address as you work, so a refresh never loses it.
