## Basics

**Is my9games.cc free? Do I need an account?**
Free, no account, no app. There is no limit on downloads.

**Does it work on a phone?**
Yes. Tap a square to search, tap covers to move them. In the tier list maker, tap a cover and then a tier.

**Where is my grid saved?**
In the page address. Refreshing keeps it; closing the tab without copying the link does not. Press **Copy link** to keep a grid for later.

## Games and covers

**I can't find a game.**
Try another way of writing it: the acronym (`kh2`), the original title, a number written differently (`ff10` / `ffx`), or add the year (`doom 2016`). The catalog has 60,000+ games from IGDB. If it is really missing, tell the site through [my9games.cc/contact](https://my9games.cc/contact).

**I got the remake instead of the original (or the other way round).**
Add a year or the word remake: `resident evil 4 2005`, `re4 remake`. Each result shows its year and platforms.

**What do the DLC / Bundle / Expanded tags mean?**
They mark add-ons, packs and re-releases. For a favorites grid, pick the entry without a tag.

## Downloading and sharing

**The download does nothing on my phone.**
Some in-app browsers (inside Instagram, TikTok, Discord…) block downloads. Open the page in Safari or Chrome. If it still fails, press and hold the image to save it.

**My link preview shows an old picture.**
Each change gives a new link. Copy the link again after your last edit. Apps like Discord cache previews, so an old message keeps its old preview.

**Can I remove hours or the site label from the image?**
Hours: untick **Show Steam hours**. The small my9games.cc label stays on every image.

**How do I post on Reddit?**
Download the image first, then press **Post on Reddit**: it opens an image post with the title filled in, and you attach the image.

## Steam

**"Game details are private"**: set Game details to Public in Steam's Privacy Settings. [Step by step](../steam-import/#step-1-make-game-details-public).

**"Steam is hiding the hours"**: untick "Always keep my total playtime private" in the same place.

**"No Steam profile found"**: paste the full profile link instead of a name. Your custom URL name is not your display name.

**I changed my privacy setting and it still fails.**
Make sure you saved the setting and that you changed **Game details**, not only "My profile". Then try again.

**Is my Steam ID stored?**
No. See [privacy on HOW TIME FLIES?](../how-time-flies/#privacy).

**How do I take my games out of the HOW TIME FLIES? ranking?**
Untick **Add my games to the HOW TIME FLIES? ranking** and import again. The ranking is recounted every hour.

**Can I add console games to a Steam grid?**
Yes. After the import, tap any square and search for the game.

## Tier lists

**How many tiers and games can I have?**
Up to 8 tiers and 100 games.

**Can I get the old S–D rows back?**
Press **Reset** for a fresh S, A, B, C, D board (this also clears your games).

## AI

**The connector doesn't show up in my chat.**
Check it is switched on for the conversation (tools or **+** menu) and that the URL is exactly `https://my9games.cc/mcp`. [Setup](../use-with-ai/).

## Still stuck?

Write through [my9games.cc/contact](https://my9games.cc/contact). Include the link to your grid or the Steam message you saw.
