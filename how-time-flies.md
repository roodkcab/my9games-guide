**HOW TIME FLIES?** is the fastest thing on my9games.cc: paste a Steam profile link and get one image of the games you have sunk the most hours into. No searching, no dragging.

**[Make yours at my9games.cc/steam →](https://my9games.cc/steam)**

## Make the image

1. Make sure your Steam **Game details** are public, then copy your profile link. Both are explained step by step in [Steam import](../steam-import/).
2. Open [my9games.cc/steam](https://my9games.cc/steam), paste the link and press **Make mine**.

![The HOW TIME FLIES? form]({{ '/assets/img/steam-form.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="704" height="251" loading="lazy"}

A few seconds later the image is there. It follows the 10-hour rule: every game you played for 10 hours or more gets a cover, at least 9 and at most 20, most played first, with the hours in the corner of each cover.

![A HOW TIME FLIES? result with sharing buttons and the rest of the library]({{ '/assets/img/steam-result.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="712" height="1085" loading="lazy"}

## Adjust it

- **More from your library** lists your other games, most played first. Tap one to add it, tap a ticked one to take it out. Up to 25 games fit (5×5).
- **Edit this grid** opens the same games in the full grid maker, where you can reorder covers, change the title, switch to the classic style or search for games that are not on Steam.

## Share it

- **Download** saves the image.
- **Share**: on a phone it opens the share sheet with the image and a short post, so you can send it to any app. On a computer it copies the post text and link; paste it next to your image.
- **Post on X** opens a ready-made post with the link.
- **Post on Reddit** opens an image post with the title filled in. On a computer the image is copied for you, so you only paste it; on a phone it goes through the share sheet.

## The ranking

Under the button is a box: **Add my games to the HOW TIME FLIES? ranking**. When it is ticked, your games and their hours join the public [HOW TIME FLIES? ranking](https://my9games.cc/steam/ranking) of what everyone plays the most.

![The HOW TIME FLIES? ranking]({{ '/assets/img/ranking.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="720" height="820" loading="lazy"}

How it is counted:

- **What goes in**: the same games as your image, meaning everything you played for 10 hours or more, at least your top 9 and at most 20.
- **Most players** counts how many people have a game in that set. **Most hours** adds up everyone's hours on it.
- **5-player threshold**: a game only appears once at least 5 players have it, so no single library can be picked out of the list.
- **One account, one entry**: importing the same Steam account again, from any device, replaces its games instead of adding them twice.
- The list is recounted every hour, so a new entry can take up to an hour to show.

## Privacy

- Your Steam ID, profile link and name are **never saved** and not written to logs.
- The ranking keeps only games and hours, filed under a one-way scrambled code so the same account counts once. The Steam ID cannot be recovered from it.
- The import result sits in a short cache for about ten minutes so a second try is quick, then it is gone.
- **Opting out**: untick the box and import again. Your games are taken out of the ranking.
- You never sign in to Steam, and you can make Game details private again as soon as you have your image.

The full policy is on the [my9games.cc privacy page](https://my9games.cc/privacy).
