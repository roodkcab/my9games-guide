You need three minutes and nine games. No sign-up, nothing to install.

**[Open the grid maker on my9games.cc →](https://my9games.cc/)**

## 1. Fill the first square

Tap any empty square, or the big yellow search bar on the homepage. A search box opens and tells you which slot you are filling ("Slot 1 of 9").

Type a game the way you would say it to a friend. The search is built for that:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `botw`, `rdr2`, `bg3` | the full title from its initials |
| `melee`, `silksong`, `new vegas` | the game behind the nickname |
| `ff7` or `ffvii` | the same game: Roman and Arabic numbers both work |
| `skyrm`, `eldin ring` | the game you meant, typo and all |
| `resident evil 4 2023` | a specific release, when a game has a remake |

![Searching "botw" puts Breath of the Wild first]({{ '/assets/img/search.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="573" height="584" loading="lazy"}

Results carry small tags such as **DLC**, **Bundle** or **Expanded**. For a favorites grid you usually want the plain entry without a tag. Click a result (or use ↑ ↓ and Enter) and the search jumps to the next empty slot, so you can type nine games in a row without touching the mouse.

## 2. Put them in order

Drag a cover onto another square to swap them. People read a grid from the top left, so that is a good spot for the game that matters most. To take a game out, hover or tap its cover and press **×**.

Not sure what the nine should be about? **🎲 Pick template** fills in a theme ("Games that made me", "My childhood games", "Games that beat me" and more) and puts a prompt in each empty square. The title box above the grid is yours to rewrite.

![The grid maker with nine games, the style switch and the action buttons]({{ '/assets/img/grid-maker.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="632" height="1277" loading="lazy"}

## 3. Choose how it looks

Under the grid:

- **Show game titles on the covers**: names and years on each cover. Leave it on if your friends might not know every box art; turn it off for a clean wall.
- **Style: Classic or Borderless**. Classic keeps your title on top and frames each cover. Borderless puts the covers edge to edge with only a small logo. [See both side by side](../borderless-and-grid-sizes/).

Want more than nine? Press **+** next to the game count. You can go up to 25.

## 4. Download the image

Press **Download image**. The site shows the two styles once more so you can switch at the last moment; pick one and a PNG lands in your downloads, 1080 pixels wide, ready for a phone screen.

![Choose a style when you download]({{ '/assets/img/download-style-picker.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="564" height="502" loading="lazy"}

## 5. Share it

Press **Copy link**. You get a short link, and when you paste it on X, Discord or iMessage the preview shows your actual grid. Anyone who opens it sees your nine and can edit their own copy.

After copying, a **Post it** row appears:

- **Post on X** opens a ready-made post with your link.
- **Post on Reddit** opens an image post with the title filled in. Attach the image you downloaded and submit.

![After Copy link: Post on X and Post on Reddit]({{ '/assets/img/share.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="620" height="220" loading="lazy"}

## Bonus: compare with friends

**Create your own ranklist** turns your grid into a shared list. Send the link; each friend adds their nine, and once two people have joined the list shows which games your group picked most.
