Every grid on my9games.cc comes in two styles and any size from 9 to 25 games. This page helps you choose before you post.

## The two styles

| | Classic | Borderless |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Big, on top | None |
| Covers | Rounded, with a gap between them | Edge to edge, no gaps |
| Game names | Optional (Show game titles on the covers) | Never |
| Branding | Small site name next to the title | A small label in one corner |
| Best for | Grids where the theme matters: "Games that made me", "My childhood" | Grids where the covers speak: a backlog, a year, a Steam library |

Switch with **Style: Classic / Borderless** under the grid. You get one more chance when you press **Download image**, and **Copy link** keeps the style you chose, so the link preview matches your picture.

## Sizes from 3×3 to 5×5

Press **+** next to the game count to add a square, **−** to remove one. The layout reflows by itself:

| Games | Layout |
|---|---|
| 9 | 3×3 |
| 10–16 | rows of 4 (16 = a full 4×4) |
| 17–25 | rows of 5 (20 = 5×4, 25 = 5×5) |

When the last row is short, it is centered. The image you download is always the same shape as the grid on screen.

The famous follow-up, "now do 10–16", is just **+** seven times. The **Now do 10-16.** template sets it up for you: 16 squares, with a prompt in each of the seven new ones ("The one that almost made your nine", "An honorable mention" and so on).

## Which one for Reddit, which one for X

**Reddit** (for example r/my9games): the image is the post, and people scroll past titles. Borderless reads as one picture in the feed and needs no explanation. Put your theme in the post title instead. A 3×3 or 5×5 is a calm portrait shape that the feed shows in full.

**X**: very tall images can be cropped in the timeline preview, and the text of your post sits right above the picture. A **classic** grid keeps the theme visible inside the image, which helps when it is reposted without your words. For bigger grids, **5×4** (20 games) is the most compact shape and is cropped the least.

**Group chats and Discord**: send the link from **Copy link** rather than the file. The preview card shows your grid, and friends can open it and make their own.

Rule of thumb: the theme matters → classic. The covers are the point → borderless.
